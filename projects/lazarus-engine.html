Lazarus Engine

An automated lead reactivation system designed to resurrect cold CRM databases through personalized multi-channel outreach campaigns.

Database Reactivation n8n Workflow GoHighLevel / CRM Multi-Channel Nurture
⚡ Automated Reactivation System · Cold Lead Recovery

⚠️ The Problem

Businesses spend heavily to acquire leads, but up to 80% go cold and sit dormant in CRMs without structured follow-up, burning potential revenue.

🎯 Example Persona

Real Estate & Service Businesses

Operators holding 1,000+ unengaged historical leads who want to unlock high-intent appointments without buying fresh ad traffic.

✅ The Solution

A multi-stage reactivation engine that segments stale CRM data, initiates conversational SMS/Email hooks, and auto-books replies onto sales calendars.

📸 Workflow Architecture Visual (Reactivation Flow) system_02_canvas.png
Execution Architecture

Step-by-Step Workflow Logic

STAGE 1: AUDIENCE SEGMENTATION
Filters CRM contacts inactive for 60+ days into segmented batches to protect domain reputation.
STAGE 2: CONVERSATIONAL HOOK
Dispatches short, low-friction, open-ended messages designed strictly to trigger replies (e.g., "Are you still looking for [X]?").
STAGE 3: RESPONSE MONITORING & BRANCHING
Monitors inbound message channels:
✓ Positive Reply: Tags contact as "Reactivated", notifies sales team, and pushes calendar booking link.
✗ No Response: Pauses outreach for 14 days before secondary soft follow-up touch.
STAGE 4: PIPELINE UPDATES
Automatically moves reactivated records into active sales stages inside the CRM.
Verification Status: Configured and verified with multi-channel delay and response handling logic.
