LeadFlow AI

An AI-powered lead intake & distribution pipeline that captures, enriches, and qualifies inbound leads automatically using n8n and OpenAI.

n8n OpenAI API Lead Qualification Webhook Integration
⚡ Automated Workflow Engine · Real-Time Intake

⚠️ The Problem

Inbound leads often wait hours for human response, while unqualified or spam form submissions clutter sales pipelines and waste valuable rep time.

🎯 Example Persona

Agencies & High-Volume Sales Teams

Growth teams receiving 50+ inbound leads daily who need immediate automated scoring and routing before handing off to account executives.

✅ The Solution

An n8n workflow that ingests webhooks in real-time, leverages OpenAI to score intent, enriches contact details, and notifies reps instantly via Slack/CRM.

📸 Workflow Architecture Visual (n8n Canvas) system_01_canvas.png
Execution Architecture

Step-by-Step Workflow Logic

STAGE 1: WEBHOOK INGESTION
Receives raw payload immediately from website contact forms or ad lead forms.
STAGE 2: AI EVALUATION
Passes input parameters to OpenAI to parse lead intent, detect project scope, and assign a fit score (0–100).
STAGE 3: CONDITIONAL ROUTING
Evaluates score threshold:
✓ High-Intent (Score ≥ 70): Dispatches instant Slack notification to sales lead & logs priority lead in CRM.
✗ Low-Intent (Score < 70): Adds record to automated email nurture queue.
STAGE 4: CRM SYNC & CONFIRMATION
Updates lead record status and sends immediate personalized receipt confirmation email to prospect.
Verification Status: Tested and validated via end-to-end webhook execution in a self-hosted n8n environment.
