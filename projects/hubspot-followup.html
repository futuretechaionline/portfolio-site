HubSpot Lead Follow-Up Automation

A timed, multi-branch HubSpot workflow that chases converted leads on a schedule — engaging responders and automatically disqualifying unengaged records.

HubSpot Workflows Email Automation CRM Logic Lead Lifecycle
⚡ Built in HubSpot's Workflow Builder · Internal CRM Logic

⚠️ The Problem

When inbound leads convert, sales reps manually send follow-ups and often forget to track delays. Unanswered leads sit untouched in the CRM, leading to high drop-off rates and inaccurate pipeline reporting.

🎯 Target Persona

B2B Sales Rep & Marketing Ops Lead

Sales Reps managing 30+ new lead inquiries weekly who need automated nurture reminders, and RevOps Leaders requiring clean lifecycle stage updates without manual entry.

✅ The Solution

A multi-stage HubSpot workflow that initiates immediate outreach, monitors response delays, dispatches timed reminders, and automatically flags non-responders as "Unqualified Lead".

hubspot-workflow-canvas.png
Execution Architecture

Step-by-Step Workflow Logic

1
Stage 1: Trigger
Contact form submission converts lifecycle stage to "Lead" in HubSpot.
2
Stage 2: Initial Outreach
Automated introduction email dispatches immediately populated with personalized contact properties.
3
Stage 3: Timed Delay & Branching
System waits 2 Days and evaluates engagement:
✓ Replied / Opened: Triggers internal team notification & assigns sales task → Workflow Ends
✗ No Reply: Dispatches Follow-up Email #1 → Waits 3 Days
4
Stage 4: Final Re-engagement
If silence persists after 3 days, dispatches Final Follow-up email and waits 3 concluding days.
5
Stage 5: Auto-Disqualification
If zero engagement occurs after final window, auto-updates contact status to "Unqualified Lead" to keep CRM data clean.
Verification Status: Configured and tested end-to-end within a HubSpot CRM Developer Sandbox environment as self-directed practice.
