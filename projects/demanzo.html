SYSTEM 05 · CLIENT PROJECT

HubSpot CRM Audit, Cleanup & Automation Optimization

A practical HubSpot CRM optimization project focused on data quality, CRM structure, lead management, workflow review, and reporting readiness.

HubSpot CRMCRM AuditData QualityLifecycle StageLead StatusWorkflow ReviewReporting
● Client project · Portfolio case study

⚠ The Problem

The existing CRM needed a structured review across Contacts, Companies, Leads, Deals, properties, lifecycle stages, Lead Status, pipelines, associations, workflows, and reporting. The goal was to identify meaningful operational gaps rather than hunt for minor issues.

◉ Project Context

Demanzo · HubSpot CRM & Automation Consultant

✓ The Solution

Audited the existing CRM structure, reviewed data quality and associations, evaluated lead qualification and sales handoff processes, reviewed automation dependencies, and documented practical recommendations for improving CRM reliability and reporting.

Execution Architecture

From CRM audit to actionable recommendations

The work followed a consultant-style flow: understand the existing environment, inspect important CRM objects and processes, classify business-impacting issues, then document and validate the recommended improvements.

1

1 · Scope the CRM

Review the existing Contacts, Companies, Leads, Deals, properties, pipelines, associations, workflows and reporting requirements within the agreed scope.

2

2 · Inspect data quality

Look for missing or inconsistent data, association gaps, property issues, lifecycle/Lead Status problems and other issues that can affect lead management or reporting.

3

3 · Review the sales process

Evaluate qualification, handoff, routing, follow-up and pipeline processes to identify manual steps, unclear ownership or process gaps.

4

4 · Review automation

Inspect relevant workflows and automation for overlapping logic, dependencies, manual steps and opportunities for safer process improvement.

5

5 · Document findings

Capture the issue, evidence, business impact, priority and recommendation so the client has a practical action plan.

6

6 · Validate recommendations

Check that proposed changes fit the existing CRM structure and avoid creating duplicate properties, conflicting logic or unnecessary automation.
