Outreach Engine

An n8n cold-email sequencing workflow that automates multi-step prospect outreach and response logging at scale.

n8n Cold Email Sequencing Logic & Delays Google Sheets
⚡ Workflow JSON Built Locally · Not Publicly Deployed

⚠️ The Problem

Manual cold outreach caps out fast. Copy-pasting individual emails to 20 prospects a day limits pipeline generation and wastes productive hours.

🎯 Target Persona

B2B Founders & SDRs

Sales teams looking to scale outbound lead generation consistently without losing personalization or paying high monthly subscription fees for heavy software.

✅ The Solution

An n8n workflow that ingests prospect lists, sequences multi-touch cold emails with calculated spacing, and logs status directly to CRM or Google Sheets.

system_04_n8n.png
Execution Architecture

Step-by-Step Workflow Logic

1
Stage 1: Ingestion
Pulls target prospect batch from Google Sheets/CSV, mapping dynamic variables like First Name and Company.
2
Stage 2: Initial Outreach
Dispatches Email 1 via SMTP/API nodes, populating personalized context and updating status to "Contacted".
3
Stage 3: Timed Delay & Conditional Follow-Up
Pauses execution for 3 to 5 days to monitor response:
✓ Replied: Halts automated sequence & alerts rep to take over manually.
✗ No Reply: Dispatches threaded Follow-up Email 2 ("Bumping this to the top of your inbox...").
4
Stage 4: Status Logging
Records final disposition and outreach history back to the database.
Honest Status: Existing as a tested JSON file in a local n8n instance — designed and built end-to-end.
