⚠️ The Problem

Manual cold outreach caps out fast. Copy-pasting the same email with small edits to 20 prospects a day is the absolute ceiling for one person working by hand, severely limiting pipeline generation.

🎯 Example Persona

B2B Founders & SDRs

Sales teams and agency founders looking to scale their outbound lead generation efforts consistently without losing personalization or paying high monthly fees for heavy outreach software.

✅ The Solution

An n8n workflow takes a prospect list, sequences a multi-step cold-email send with calculated spacing between touches, and logs the status per contact — so outreach volume isn't capped by typing speed.

Execution Architecture

Step-by-Step Workflow Logic

STAGE 1: INGESTION
Workflow pulls a batch of leads from a target prospect list (Google Sheets/CSV), mapping dynamic variables like `First Name` and `Company`.
STAGE 2: INITIAL OUTREACH
Dispatches Email 1 via SMTP/API nodes, inserting dynamic variables to maintain a personalized feel at scale. Updates CRM/Sheet status to "Contacted".
STAGE 3: TIMED DELAY NODE
Execution pauses for 3 to 5 days to give the prospect time to read and respond without aggressive spamming.
STAGE 4: CONDITIONAL FOLLOW-UP
Workflow resumes, checking for inbox replies. If no reply is detected, it automatically threads and dispatches Follow-up Email 2 ("Bumping this to the top of your inbox...").
Honest Status: This workflow exists as a built JSON file, tested in a local n8n instance. It has not been deployed to a live server or run against a real prospect list at scale — described here as built, not shipped.
