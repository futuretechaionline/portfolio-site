HubSpot Lead Follow-Up Automation

A timed, multi-branch HubSpot workflow that chases a converted lead for a reply — and automatically disqualifies the ones who never respond.

HubSpot WorkflowsEmail AutomationCRM Logic
Built in HubSpot's workflow tool · no public demo (internal CRM logic)

The problem

A contact converts into a lead. Someone has to email them, then remember to check back in a few days, send a follow-up if there's no reply, try once more, and eventually mark the lead dead if it goes nowhere. Reps forget steps, and CRM records go stale.

The solution

A HubSpot workflow runs the entire chase sequence on a timer with branching logic — reply or open triggers an internal handoff, silence triggers the next follow-up, and total silence auto-updates the CRM. No manual tracking.

Architecture

The follow-up logic, step by step

Trigger
Contact is converted to Lead in HubSpot.
Action
Automated initial email sends immediately.
Wait 2 days → Branch: did they reply?
✓ Reply received → notify internal team with contact + email details → workflow ends
✗ No reply → send Follow-up 1 → wait 3 days
Branch: reply or email opened in that window?
✓ Yes → stop workflow, notify internal team
✗ No → send Final Follow-up → wait 3 days (same logic)
Branch: still no reply?
✓ Reply at any point → notify internal team
✗ No reply at all → auto-update CRM: mark contact "Unqualified Lead"
Status: Built and configured in a HubSpot workflow environment as self-directed practice. Not run against live client contact volume.
